X3 Stage-D red-team (A) — SHA-512 detector sensitivity proof
============================================================
DELIVERABLE A: prove the Zknh SHA-512 KAT/per-op checks are SENSITIVE (before
this, only sha256 had a committed seed — x3_sha256_rotate_seed.patch; the
sha512 ops were exercised by extzknh.S + rv32uzknh/sha512.S but NO seed proved
they would BITE — the jvtmask-gap concern).

SEED (x3_redteam_sha512_shift.patch): change the second shift term of the
sha512sig0l ALU arm (hdl/common/vesta/alu.vhd, alu_control "1000100") from the
ratified `(a >> 7)` to `(a >> 6)`:

    when "1000100" => -- sha512sig0l: (a>>1)^(a>>7)^(a>>8) ^ ...
        if ENABLE_ZKN then
            ResultSignal <= std_logic_vector(
                shift_right(unsigned(a),  1) xor
-               shift_right(unsigned(a),  7) xor
+               shift_right(unsigned(a),  6) xor
                shift_right(unsigned(a),  8) xor ...

sha512sig0l is the low-half of SHA-512 Sigma0; a wrong shift corrupts the
per-op golden (0xb192c77c) AND the 64-bit ror64 reconstruction (Sigma0-lo =
0x6c4f1aa1). Both the directed per-op CK and the KAT `bne`-to-fail fire ->
a0 = 0xDEADBEEF -> tb "TEST FAILED" via a0_reached_fail, BOUNDED (not a timeout).

WHY THIS PROVES SENSITIVITY (contrast with jvtmask): every sha512 op in both
extzknh.S and rv32uzknh/sha512.S carries a FIXED golden constant, so a single
formula-bit change is caught by the op's own check. The sha256 arms are
untouched -> sha256 PASSES (the seed is TARGETED, not a blanket break).
