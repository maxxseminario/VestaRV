==========================================================
DELIVERABLE C: complement to the existing UNDER-run seed (x3_zicboz_partial.patch,
which stops at 15 words -> tail word non-zero). This seed OVER-runs by ONE word
(writes 17 words: cboz_idx 0..16, so word 16 lands at block_base+64) and proves
the AFTER-block GUARD word catches the word immediately past the 64-byte block.

GUARD COVERAGE FINDING: both extzicboz.S and shcboz.S already set an AFTER guard
sentinel (0x22222222) at offset +64 = the word immediately past the block, and
check it is untouched. That guard EXACTLY covers the over-run word, so NO test
strengthening was required for C (the existing guard is sufficient).

SEED (x3_redteam_zicboz_overrun.patch): move the CBOZ store-burst terminal
boundary up by one word — a coherent off-by-one where the whole retire boundary
shifts from CBOZ_WORDS-1 to CBOZ_WORDS (hdl/common/vesta/vesta.vhd), 3 coupled edits:
  - cboz_idx range   0..CBOZ_WORDS-1  ->  0..CBOZ_WORDS   (allow idx=16)
  - cboz_seq_proc increment guard  cboz_idx /= CBOZ_WORDS-1  ->  /= CBOZ_WORDS
  - CBOZ_GAP retire test  cboz_idx = CBOZ_WORDS-1  ->  = CBOZ_WORDS
Net effect: the sequencer issues 17 full-word zero stores instead of 16; word 16
writes block_base+64, clobbering the AFTER guard. Sequence still terminates
(retires at idx=16) -> BOUNDED, no watchdog hang.
