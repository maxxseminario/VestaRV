%% HAND-DRAWN for this deck.  How long an electrode stays in contact with
%% the sample, on a log time axis, for the three deployment rungs, with real
%% devices as the anchors:

%%   wearable    Dexcom G7 10 / 15-day and FreeStyle Libre 3 14-day CGM sensors
%%               (enzymatic electrochemical, interstitial fluid) [27]; wound
%%               patch with a multiplexed electrochemical array, preclinical
%%               in vivo (Sani et al. 2023) [28]; week-long in vivo E-AB drug
%%               measurement (Son et al. 2026) [29]
%%   implant     Eversense 365, subcutaneous CGM cleared for 365 days [27]
%% Electrode drift and fouling grow with time in the sample [24-26]; that is
