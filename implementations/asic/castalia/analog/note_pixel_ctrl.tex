\subsubsection{Control Loop}\label{sss:pixel-ctrl}

The commanded reference potential $v_{\mathrm{ref}}$ is swept
\SIrange{0}{2.5}{\volt}; Table~\ref{tab:px-ctrl-stability} gives the resulting
loop stability and Table~\ref{tab:px-ctrl-tracking} the tracking error. Loop
gain exceeds \SI{71.8}{\decibel} and crossover exceeds \SI{21.2}{\mega\hertz}
at every corner, both well past the \SI{60}{\decibel}/\SI{60}{\degree} limits
entered on the bench; the worst tracking error stays at or below
\SI{0.814}{\milli\volt} over the fixed \SIrange{0.4}{2.1}{\volt} evaluation
window at every corner. Table~\ref{tab:px-ctrl-range} and
Table~\ref{tab:px-ctrl-drive} give the compliance: the reference electrode
tracks over \SIrange{0.054}{2.44}{\volt} (a \SI{10}{\milli\volt} error
criterion, offset-inclusive) and the loop delivers \SI{\pm 52.5}{\micro\ampere}
into the cell at every corner, read through the TIA feedback probe since no
separate truth-current source is active on this bench.
