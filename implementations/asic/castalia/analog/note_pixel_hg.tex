\paragraph{Finite-loop-gain error away from the operational electrode.}
Table~\ref{tab:px-hg-demo} sweeps a small band around zero at all four gain
taps with the electrode held at the stiff \SI{10}{\kilo\ohm} cell used for the
control-loop bench. The 140, 315 and \SI{560}{\kilo\ohm} rows are not the
accuracy of those taps -- \SI{10}{\kilo\ohm} is not the electrode those taps
are designed against -- but they isolate the mechanism: the zero-crossing
slope error tracks $-1/T$ within \SI{4}{\percent} at every tap, so the growth
in worst-case error with $R_f$ is finite closed-loop gain, not a distortion
that appears only at high gain.
