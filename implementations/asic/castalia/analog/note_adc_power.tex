\subsubsection{Supply Current and Conversion Energy}\label{sss:adc-power}

Total power spans \SIrange{38.5}{48.1}{\micro\watt} over the input sweep about a
mean of \SI{43.8}{\micro\watt}, with no monotonic dependence on input level
(Table~\ref{tab:adc-power}). The analog \SI{2.5}{\volt} rail dominates at
\SIrange{11.8}{15.4}{\micro\ampere}; the digital rails average
\SI{2.30}{\micro\ampere} at \SI{2.5}{\volt} and \SI{3.45}{\micro\ampere} at
\SI{1.0}{\volt}. Over the \SI{1.15}{\micro\second} conversion that is
\SIrange{44.3}{55.3}{\pico\joule} per conversion, mean
\SI{50.4}{\pico\joule}.

Each current is sampled at the same settled point as the code, not averaged over
the acquisition and conversion phases, and the energy column is that power held
for the full conversion period. A converter whose instantaneous current differs
between phases will not integrate to these figures.
