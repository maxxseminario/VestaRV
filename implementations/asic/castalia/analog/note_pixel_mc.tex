\subsubsection{Monte Carlo: Zero-Current Offset}\label{sss:pixel-mc}

200 samples, \textbf{mismatch only}, nominal process at \SI{25}{\celsius}, the
\SI{10}{\kilo\ohm} electrode, one 200-sample sub-run at each of the four gain
taps. The reference-electrode offset $\sigma(v_{\mathrm{re,zero}}) =
\SI{5.72}{\milli\volt}$ (Table~\ref{tab:px-mc-rezero}) is the clean,
gain-independent statistic: it is the control loop's own tracking offset
carried to the electrode, identical to five significant figures at all four
gain taps. The output-referred current offset $\sigma(i_{\mathrm{zero}})$ is
nearly gain-independent in its own right, \SIrange{0.729}{0.843}{\micro\ampere}
across the four taps (Table~\ref{tab:px-mc-izero-allgain}) -- consistent with
$\sigma(i_{\mathrm{zero}}) \approx \sigma(v_{\mathrm{re,zero}})/R_{\mathrm{cell}}$.
Referred to the nominal \SI{2.441}{\milli\volt} ADC LSB the same offset
current scales with $R_f$: $\sigma
\approx 12/43/96/167$~LSB at 35/140/315/\SI{560}{\kilo\ohm}
(Figure~\ref{fig:px-mc-izero-35k}, Figure~\ref{fig:px-mc-izero-560k}). At
\SI{560}{\kilo\ohm} some samples reach the output rails
(Table~\ref{tab:px-mc-izero}) -- this bounds the range a vcm-mux offset
calibration must cover. Applying the same \SI{5.72}{\milli\volt} loop offset
to the operational \SI{1}{\mega\ohm} electrode of
Table~\ref{tab:px-hg1m-acc} predicts $\approx 1.3$~LSB at
\SI{560}{\kilo\ohm} -- a channel-to-channel spread on top of, not instead of,
the corner-level finite-gain error of that table. Both LSB figures are on the
nominal step; the converter's measured step is \SI{1.591}{\milli\volt}
(Table~\ref{tab:adc-transfer}), so multiply by \num{1.535} to read them as
converter codes.
