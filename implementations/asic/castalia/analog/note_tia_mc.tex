\subsubsection{Monte Carlo}\label{sss:tia-mc}

Statistical characterisation of the transimpedance stage over process and mismatch
together (\texttt{castalia\_pm\_25}): 200 samples, low-discrepancy sampling, seed 12345,
\SI{37}{\celsius}, both gain taps, into the cell of Figure~\ref{fig:randles-cell} with
$R_s = \SI{100}{\ohm}$, $R_{\mathrm{ct}} = \SI{1}{\mega\ohm}$,
$C_{\mathrm{dl}} = \SI{100}{\nano\farad}$ and $C_f = \SI{22}{\pico\farad}$.
Schematic-level, no extracted parasitics.

Phase margin is above the \SI{45}{\degree} criterion on every sample at both taps
(Tables~\ref{tab:tia-mc-stab35k} and~\ref{tab:tia-mc-stab560k},
Figure~\ref{fig:tia-mc-pm560k}). Gain peaking is below \SI{2}{\decibel} on
\SI{87.7}{\percent} of samples at maximum gain and on every sample at minimum gain
(Figure~\ref{fig:tia-mc-peak560k}). Input offset is within \(\pm\SI{5}{\milli\volt}\) on
\SI{74.0}{\percent} of samples at minimum gain and \SI{54.7}{\percent} at maximum gain
(Table~\ref{tab:tia-mc-offset}, Figure~\ref{fig:tia-mc-voff}); over four channels the
corresponding figures are \SI{30.0}{\percent} and \SI{9.0}{\percent}. Input-referred
current noise is within \SI{2}{\nano\ampere} on every sample, with a maximum of
\SI{1276}{\pico\ampere}.

\paragraph{Conditions on the statistics.} $\sigma$ is a combined process and mismatch
figure and is not comparable with a mismatch-only $\sigma$. Skewed quantities are
reported as 1st and 99th percentiles with the extreme sample rather than mean
$\pm\,3\sigma$; the offset distribution is close to Gaussian and mean $\pm\,3\sigma$ is
given for it. Statistics are taken over the samples whose DC operating point places the
output within the linear range of the transfer of Equation~(\ref{eq:tia-transfer}): 173
of 200 at \SI{35}{\kilo\ohm} and 179 of 200 at \SI{560}{\kilo\ohm}. The remaining
samples reach the sourcing limit of the class~A output stage
(Section~\ref{ss:tiaamp}) below \SI{1}{\micro\ampere} of electrode current and are
excluded; the DC transfer, integral non-linearity and signal bandwidth of
Table~\ref{tab:tia-transfer} are characterised over process corners only.
