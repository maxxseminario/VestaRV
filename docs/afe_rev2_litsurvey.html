1 · Ovarian 4-plex: circuits-level readiness

Verdict: the architecture already covers every circuit requirement the published assays impose. Nothing new needs to be designed — three open integration items, all already on the books, stand between "capable" and "ready." A fourth (vref DAC sharing) was decided 2026-08-16: one vref DAC per channel.

1.3 · Why ovarian, not another cancer

Four markers, not four locations. One blood draw is split across the four sites; each site's WE carries a different capture probe (anti-CA-125, anti-HE4, …) and all four are swept simultaneously. The channels are four parallel assays on the same sample. (The spatial reading — 4 sites = 4 places — belongs to the other candidates: wound bed, culture wells. Channels can also buy redundancy: 2 markers × 2 replicates for on-die QC.)

Why any panel must be multi-marker: no single ovarian marker works. CA-125 is elevated in only ~50–60% of early-stage disease and rises with benign conditions (endometriosis, menstruation, pregnancy) — alone it both misses cancers and false-alarms. Combined panels reach 98.2% sensitivity / 98.7% specificity. The diagnostic power is in the pattern across markers — so a cancer that requires fusion makes 4 simultaneous channels + NPU fusion load-bearing rather than decorative.

Three reasons stack for ovarian specifically:

  1. The unmet need is unusually clean. No approved screening test exists; USPSTF recommends against CA-125+ultrasound screening (specificity too poor — false positives drive unnecessary surgery). ~70% present at Stage III/IV; 5-year survival >90% (Stage I) vs. <30% (late). The panel targets a hole where nothing currently exists.
  2. Algorithmic marker fusion is already clinically accepted for this exact cancer. ROMA — an FDA-cleared lab test — is an algorithm fusing CA-125 + HE4 + menopausal status. The pitch is "ROMA extended to four markers, computed on-die at point of care": extending a regulator-blessed precedent, not inventing an unvalidated concept.
  3. The electrochemical stepping stones exist. Published 2-plex (CA125+HE4) and 3-plex (HE4/AFP/CA-125) immunosensors with currents inside the chip envelope, all read sequentially on benchtop instruments. Four markers, simultaneous, on one SoC is an incremental extension with low assay-science risk and high systems novelty.
AlternativeWhy it loses the headline
BreastMammography screening exists and works, and ASCO explicitly does not recommend the serum markers (CA 15-3, CEA, HER2-ECD) for screening or early detection — they only track metastatic disease. Honest positioning is recurrence monitoring in survivors: real, but "track a known cancer" is a smaller story than "screen where nothing can." Also the most-published electrochemical panel space → least assay novelty. Kept as the mature fallback.
Pancreatic#2 pick (worse survival, also no screening test) but the best panels are miRNA-based — nucleic-acid amplification chemistry, long incubations. Held as phase-2.

1.4 · Use model — cartridge first, implant as the end state

The power architecture (per-tile gating, harvested boot) was built for an implant. The application ladder keeps every rung publishable while walking toward it:

RungForm / chemistryWhy it worksVenue
1 · POC cartridge (now)Disposable SPE strip, serum drop; single-shot sandwich DPV; NPU risk score on-die.Single-use chemistry is fine — the strip is disposable. This is the bench demo that needs only the §1.2 integration items.BioCAS → TBioCAS; Biosens. Bioelectron.
2 · Wearable / microneedle ISFReagentless E-AB aptamer probes, continuous SWV; calibration-free cSWV decomposition on the NPU.Sandwich assays are one-shot (bind–wash–label); continuous operation forces the switch to regenerable aptamer receptors. 3 · Implantable sentinel (end state)E-AB 4-plex + EIS electrode-health monitor; battery-free, duty-cycled, alert-only telemetry.See below — population and failure modes chosen to make the implant defensible.Nature BME-class

Who gets an implant. Not general screening — implanting healthy women is unjustifiable. Two populations make the risk/benefit work:

  • Post-treatment recurrence surveillance. ~80% of advanced ovarian patients relapse; a CA-125 rise precedes clinical relapse by months, and current practice samples it by blood draw every 2–3 months. An implanted sentinel interrogating daily closes that gap — and "recurrence sentinel" is a continuous-monitoring story, which is what an implant is actually good at.
  • High-risk carriers (BRCA1/2) deferring prophylactic oophorectomy. Surveillance today (CA-125 + TVUS) demonstrably fails this population; an implanted panel is the research-grade alternative framing.

What the implant changes on-chip.

  • EIS is promoted from optional to essential. Foreign-body encapsulation drift is the dominant chronic failure mode; the engine's native galvanostatic biphasic Randles extraction (§3.3) tracks Rs/Rct/Cdl per electrode and feeds the NPU's drift correction. The "spare" capability becomes load-bearing.
  • Power model is the design's home turf. Gate all four tiles; wake one channel at a time; one SWV sweep per channel per wake; NPU classifies on-die; only the risk score leaves the body. No raw-waveform streaming is precisely the harvested-power (NFC/inductive) budget. No battery means no battery-replacement surgery.
  • Aptamer SAM lifetime is the clock. Week-scale in-vivo operation is demonstrated (XNA E-AB); month-scale is unsolved. Low duty cycle extends SAM life — a published motivation, and exactly what per-tile gating implements. This is the field's open problem, not ours alone; owning part of it is publishable.
  • Sensing site is an open biology question. Subcutaneous ISF is the conservative choice (protein levels correlate with serum but lag); intraperitoneal is the aggressive one — proximal to disease, and peritoneal-fluid CA-125 runs higher than serum. Decide with the clinical collaborator; the chip is agnostic.

Consequence for sequencing: nothing in the implant end state invalidates the cartridge-first plan — same channels, same waveforms, same NPU. The chemistry swap (sandwich → E-AB) and the EIS promotion are rung-2/3 work, and each rung is a paper.

2 · Survey I — Cancer biosensing

2.1 · Multiplexed protein panels (antibody / aptamer / MIP)

Single markers fail clinically: CA-125 misses early ovarian disease and false-positives on benign conditions; CA19-9 is blind in ~10% Lewis-negative non-secretors and elevated in benign biliary obstruction. Panels win: a serum multi-marker ovarian signature reaches 98.2% sensitivity / 98.7% specificity. Transduction is sandwich/aptamer DPV-SWV peak current or enzyme-label amperometry; MIP sensors trade the biological receptor for imprinted-cavity capacitance/peak shifts and better shelf life.

  • Graphene-oxide-amplified simultaneous PSA/AFP/CA-125: LODs 86 fg/mL, 14 fg/mL, 0.0019 U/mL (Talanta ~2021).
  • 7-plex tumor-marker chip — the seminal multiplex precedent (Wu et al., Anal. Chem. 2006).
  • Duplex CA15-3+HER2-ECD on SPCE (Sens. Actuators B 2017); triplex MUC-1/CA15-3/HER2 at 0.5/0.2/0.5 ng/mL (Front. Med. Technol. 2024 review).
  • Ovarian: 3-plex HE4/AFP/CA-125 magneto-immunosensor (Electrochim. Acta 2023); CA125+HE4 POC (Biomed. Microdevices 2023); field reviews PMC11587864, PMC12024575.
  • MIP CA15-3 POC sensor (2024); translation-readiness review (PMC12944742).

Chip relevance: essentially all published "multiplex" panels are one multi-electrode strip read sequentially by a benchtop potentiostat. Four native simultaneous channels + on-die fusion is the unclaimed axis.

2.3 · Label-free EIS cancer sensing

Aptasensor Rct assays (CEA LOD 0.085 ng/mL, Front. Chem. 2021; PSA, PubMed 32649855) sweep 0.1 Hz–100 kHz with the diagnostic semicircle at 1–100 kHz — above the engine's 2 kHz-family baseband. Cell-based work (ECIS proliferation/invasion/drug response, 2024 review; SKOV-3 ovarian invasion at 4 kHz, AJP-Cell; CTC capture platforms, Sens. Diagn. 2024) sits at 1–10 kHz — marginal. The closest competitor to the whole concept: Ultradense Electrochemical Chip + ML for Anticancer Drug Screening, ACS Sensors 2025 — ML off-chip.

2.5 · ML in the published record

All off-chip. Voltammogram classification of lung-cancer plasma: mean accuracy 0.73 — unsolved, room to improve. AI-optimized aptasensors cut false rates from 15–20% to 5–10% (2024). EIS-spectrum deep learning for oral/cervical tissue exists (Sci. Rep. 2025, PMC8713385) and is methodologically transferable. On-die panel fusion + harvested power remains unclaimed.

3 · Survey II — EIS & impedance diagnostics

3.1 · Cross-cutting caveat

The engine is galvanostatic. Tissue/bioimpedance work injects current and senses voltage — a direct match. Faradaic Rct biosensing is conventionally potentiostatic: DC bias at the redox couple's formal potential with 5–10 mV AC. Without a DC-bias hold at the WE, the aptasensor/immunosensor category degrades; galvanostatic EIS is standard in battery/corrosion work, so adaptation is defensible but must be scoped. Also: 160 nA–15 µA must map to ≤5–10 mV of interface swing for linearity — range scheduling per assay.

3.3 · Cell culture, bacteria, implants — the good fits

  • TEER / organ-on-chip: 4 wells = 4 channels is the throughput bottleneck the field names; continuous TEER every 6 min through proliferation (2023); canonical integrated-electrode OOC (Lab Chip 2017). Spot-check ~1 kHz is native; full 0.1 Hz–1 MHz spectroscopy is not. Current-mode TEER (=ΔV/I) is directly compatible.
  • ECIS: 1–64 kHz, small-WE asymmetric electrodes; cancer drug-response phenotyping established (JAST 2018). Low-coverage high-Z wells may fall below the 160 nA floor.
  • Rapid AST: iFAST delivers susceptibility >35 h faster than culture (Nat. Commun. 2020; clinical UTI 2025). Chip niche = growth-based bulk-impedance mode, 4 parallel drug arms over 30–80 min; the single-cell 11–20 MHz cytometric mode is out of reach.
  • Biofilm: best frequency match found — real-time tracking at 1.26 kHz, <10 CFU/mL, growth = −22–25% impedance over 24 h, antimicrobial reversal +14–41% (JIMB 2023); Rct +60 kΩ (S. aureus) / +90 kΩ (S. epidermidis) (pathogen-on-chip). Low-duty-cycle monitoring = the power-gating story.
  • Implantable electrode health: galvanostatic biphasic 10–100 µA Randles extraction is the native published method (UCLA EMBC 2014: Rs 1.8–2 kΩ, Rct 15 kΩ, Cdl 176 nF) — a "free" secondary use of the engine on its own electrodes.
  • Sepsis panels: DETecT 2.0 — 8 biomarkers from 100 µL whole blood in 5 min, r>0.87 vs. Luminex (PMC9471994). Faradaic caveat applies.
  • Hydration/BIA: compartment-resolved MF-BIA needs 5 kHz–1 MHz (PNAS 2025) — single-frequency grade only. Deprioritize.

3.4 · The low-frequency defense

Restriction to sub-2 kHz is not disqualifying: low-frequency-only EIS (~100 Hz–5 kHz) is independently diagnostic for extracellular/membrane-dominated properties (Electronics 2025 review); Barrett's-esophagus screening used low-frequency-only bioimpedance; single-frequency 50 kHz BIA is clinically accepted. Rct from a truncated Nyquist arc via constrained fit is a known workaround. "What cancer-relevant impedance biology lives below 2 kHz" is itself a paper-shaped systems question.

4 · Survey III — Wearable & implantable panels

4.1 · Sweat multi-analyte

Field founded by Gao et al., Nature 2016 (glucose+lactate amperometric, Na⁺/K⁺ potentiometric); CF chloride via iontophoresis (PNAS 2017, npj Digit. Med. 2026); cortisol via aptamer/MIP SWV (Biosens. Bioelectron. 2023, Adv. Mater. 2024 review). Market $4.4B → $13.5B by 2034. Hard gap: Na⁺/K⁺/Cl⁻/pH are potentiometric ISEs — the channels are TIA-based amperometric; a CF-chloride pitch needs a high-Z buffer mode that does not exist this rev. Glucose/lactate/cortisol-SWV subsets fit natively. Sweat-rate normalization and motion-artifact rejection are the standard on-chip-ML jobs (BioGAP-Ultra lineage).

4.2 · Therapeutic drug monitoring (E-AB)

Plaxco-school electrochemical aptamer sensors: real-time in-vivo vancomycin/doxorubicin (Sci. Adv. 2023 microneedle), daunorubicin PK (Biosens. Bioelectron. 2024), week-long XNA sensors (2025), closed-loop dosing agenda (ACS Sensors 2019, Nat. Rev. Bioeng. 2025). Interrogation: SWV ~10–500 Hz, tens of nA–low µA on microneedle electrodes — the 560 kΩ tap's home range. Calibration-free cSWV (dual electron-transfer-rate decomposition, PMC12014223) is compute the NPU can take on-die; an FT-impedance E-AB interrogation mode (PMC10463274) maps onto the EIS engine. Multi-drug simultaneous panels are unaddressed. Risks: aptamer SAM lifetime; chemo deployments carry the hardest regulatory path.

4.3 · Continuous metabolic / critical care

ICU lactate still sampled at ≥6 h intervals (Biosensors 2024); bioresorbable deep-tissue lactate (Nat. Commun. 2026). Commercial state: Abbott Libre Duo = first dual glucose+ketone CGM (CE mark May 2026); Biolinq Shine cleared for glucose with lactate/ketone on a staged roadmap. A 4-simultaneous-channel triad + EIS encapsulation-drift monitor is ahead of both. All-enzymatic-amperometric: zero architecture changes. Adjacent: kidney-graft rejection monitoring — the Science 2023 Northwestern implant is thermal-led; EIS tissue-patch work (PMC11118035) is the impedance route.

4.4 · Implantable / ingestible

GI capsules validate the venue tier: PillTrek (Nat. Electron. 2025) and gut redox profiling (Nat. Electron. 2025). Orthopedic-implant infection: quantitative EIS biofilm literature (§3.3) + DET-lactate surgical-site sensing (Biosens. Bioelectron. 2024) + monitor-and-kill Ti implants (ACS AMI 2023); no product ships with detection electronics. Smart sutures: PEDOT NADH sensing (Macromol. Biosci. 2023), pH-sensing bioelectronic sutures (AFM 2024). FSCV dopamine ruled out: 400 V/s ramps need ~100–200 kHz sampling during the sweep (FSCV fundamentals) — almost certainly beyond the SAR; DPV-mode neurochemistry is the realistic fallback.

4.5 · Environmental / agricultural

The best bring-up demo: 4-metal anodic stripping voltammetry (As/Cd/Pb multiplexed, PMC8892198) with ML deconvolution of overlapping Cd/Pb/Cu/Zn peaks already demonstrated off-chip (Anal. Chim. Acta 2022). Bipolar current is structurally required (cathodic deposit, anodic strip) — the one application where rev-2's defining feature is mandatory, not merely useful. No biology, no IRB, lowest risk; lowest prestige ceiling. The UNL plant-wearables link (Markvicka group, Device 2024) is mechanical/microclimate sensing — a packaging partner, not an electrochemical precedent.

5.2 · White space

(a) Multi-channel simultaneous potentiostat array + on-chip ML: no match found. (b) Voltammetry+EIS dual-mode: commodity at 1 channel (AD5940), unpublished as 4 simultaneous sites + shared engine. (c) Harvested-power electrochemical SoC: passive systems only. No surveyed work combines even two axes; Castalia's built spec sits at the three-way intersection, plus the RISC-V/NPU compute story.
