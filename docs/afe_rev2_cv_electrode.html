1 · What Fig. 4 is today (provenance)

ISCAS27 Fig. 4 (docs/publications/iscas27-castalia/Figures/CV_Sim.pdf, label fig:cv_sim) is byte-identical to the ISCAS26 myshkin figure of the same name: a transient of the rev-1 PMOS-mirror potentiostat (potentiostat_pmos_sch4lay_upd) performing a 0→2→0 V triangular sweep over 3 s. The load is effectively resistive: icell is a straight ramp 0→0.75 μA with no redox peaks and no hysteresis. The ISCAS26 text uses it to claim linearity (NRMSE 0.025 %), and notes mirror clipping above 2 V. Fig. 3 (fig:envelope) contains no simulation data at all — the power/latency staircase is analytic, typed directly into the TikZ \addplot coordinates.

Two reasons Fig. 4 must be regenerated, not re-captioned: (1) it shows no electrochemistry — a chemical is identified by its peak potential and quantified by its peak height, and this trace has neither; (2) it is the unidirectional rev-1 front end, while the paper's channel is the rev-2 bipolar grounded-WE design whose whole point is cathodic + anodic sensing.

2 · Why a linear Randles cell cannot make this figure

The Randles cell (Rs + Rct∥Cdl, optionally + Warburg) is a small-signal linear network. Under a triangular sweep every branch current is a linear functional of the excitation: the response is a ramp (Rct path) plus a constant capacitive offset that flips sign at the vertices (Cdl·dV/dt) — exactly the featureless shape in the current Fig. 4. Varying R/C per chemical changes slopes, never peak positions. No linear network produces a voltammetric peak.

The peak is created by two nonlinearities acting together: Butler–Volmer kinetics (faradaic current exponential in overpotential, onset at the analyte's E0′) and diffusional depletion (surface concentration collapses as current flows, so the current peaks and then falls as t−1/2). Both are required: BV alone with fixed surface concentration gives a sigmoid, not a peak. Hence the model is the Randles topology (Rs, Cdl kept as linear elements) with the Rct branch replaced by a Verilog-A element implementing BV + a finite-difference 1-D diffusion layer. The Randles cell remains the correct model for the EIS mode (afe_rev2_eis.html) — EIS is small-signal, CV is not.

Per-chemical parameterization then means physical constants, each citable: E0′ (peak position — identity), n, k0, α (reversibility/shape), D and Cbulk (peak height — concentration), A (electrode), Cdl, Rs (matrix/electrode).

3.1 · Validation (model 3)

  • Randles–Ševčík: +0.08…+1.86 % over 25–400 mV/s (first cathodic scan); √v scaling 4.07 vs 4.00. Residual fully attributed: Cdl ablation reproduces Cdl·v/ip to two decimals; purely faradaic residual is a flat −0.25…−0.45 % discretisation bias.
  • ΔEp = 57.04 mV with Rs,Cdl→0, matching Nicholson's reversible 2.218·RT/F = 56.9 mV; the 59–65 mV at Rs=500 Ω is iR distortion, growing with v as it must.
  • Kinetics knob behaves per Matsuda–Ayabe: k0=0.1 cm/s is the reversible default; k0=10−3 gives ΔEp=167 mV / ip−15.5 % — the knob for sluggish real wound-marker electrodes.
  • Grid: nseg≥16 sound, default 28 (first-order convergence); timestep not the limit. Report cycle 1 against R–S (cycle 2 sits 3–5 % low from residual surface R — physics, not error); plot cycle 2.

3.2 · Concentration vs AFE range (A=1 mm2, 50 mV/s)

0.02 mM → 37 nA (N=319 range, 45 LSB) … 1 mM → 1.35 μA (N=63, 336 LSB) … 10 mM → 13.2 μA (N=0, 166 LSB). Full table: electrode_models/conc.json. Below ~0.1 mM the peak is buried in double-layer charging at 50 mV/s (charging = 37 % of a 27 nA peak) — a real design limit worth one sentence in the paper.

3.3 · Verdict and traps

Model 3 (va/bv_diff.va) is the basis for per-chemical CV figures, one instance per redox couple (model 4 is N parallel instances, no new code). Only candidate that peaks at all; validated <2 %; every distinguishing parameter (E0′, n, α, k0, D, C) is a citable physical quantity. Keep models 1/2 as the left-hand panels of plots/compare_v050.png — the quantitative argument for retiring the resistive placeholder. Use bv_diff when comparing to closed forms, bv_diff_nl when the point is the unstirred film over a wound sensor; state δ in the caption either way.
  • Zero convergence errors across ~60 runs with plain conservative settings; robustness probes to η=±12 V all passed. $limexp()/clamps kept as belt-and-braces — do not claim CV models "need" convergence aids in this fixture.
  • constants.vams lacks Faraday and Avogadro (`P_NA is a compile error); CODATA-2018 values hardcoded.
  • Concentrations normalised by 10−6 before becoming node voltages — raw mol/cm3 is below vabstol and solves to noise.
  • Sign: models emit anodic positive (IUPAC / what the TIA measures); Bard & Faulkner take cathodic positive — one flip, marked SIGN FLIP in source. Spectre -log is parsed as an input file; use +log. Working python (numpy/matplotlib) is /opt/mentor/calibre/aoj_cal/bin/python3, not /usr/bin/python3.
  • Each .va header maps every equation to its source (B&F Eq. 3.3.11 faradaic law, 4.4.2/4.4.29 diffusion + flux BC, 6.2.19 R–Š; Feldberg 1969 box method; Britz & Strutwolf Ch. 3/7; Nicholson 1965; Matsuda & Ayabe 1955) — full mapping table in §8.

4 · Biomarker panel and parameters (literature survey complete 2026-08-21)

Recommended panel: pyocyanin (−0.27 V, cathodic) · H2O2-via-GOx/Prussian-Blue (0.0 V, cathodic) · uric acid (+0.38 V, anodic) · ascorbate (+0.40 V, anodic) — the only four-analyte set simultaneously inside ±0.8 V and 0.4 nA–40 μA across full clinical ranges. Spans 6 nA–4.6 μA (three decades — exercises the programmable Rf, not one gain code). Bipolarity is forced by the chemistry: pyocyanin's useful peak IS cathodic, and PB-mediated H2O2 at 0.0 V is the only in-window route selective against urate (direct anodic H2O2 at +0.6 V is co-oxidised by the panel's own uric acid) — rev-1's unidirectional front end could not run this panel at all. Clinically coherent: infection / metabolic state / chronicity-necrosis / antioxidant status. Stated weakness (keep it): uric acid and ascorbate sit 20 mV apart — the textbook unresolvable pair on bare carbon, which is the argument for per-channel functionalised electrodes.

Caveats that must survive into the paper: no k0 in cm/s exists on carbon for any of the four — all are fitted to reproduce ΔEp or Epa+dEp/dlog v (a genuine literature gap, not a search failure); pyocyanin is adsorption-controlled (bracket with a Langmuir term); a fixed −0.4…+0.8 V sweep covers all four across pH 7.4–8.5 (uric acid moves ~65 mV toward zero at 8.5 — pH works for us); Randles–Ševčík validity at 1 mm2: diffusion layer ≤ 0.25·r at 100 mV/s (semi-infinite planar holds) but reaches 0.55·r at 20 mV/s — justify simulating at 100 mV/s on this basis (also resolves the §5.3 timescale question: 100 mV/s over −0.4…+0.8 V = 24 s round trip). Two more: the uric-acid single-peak model may fail above pH ~8 — a second oxidation peak appears in alkaline phosphate (Tyagi & Dryhurst, Bioelectrochem. Bioenerg. 13:343, 1984) and exudate reaches 8.9; and a known literature trap: “3 % H2O2 = 975 μM” (Zhu 2017) is off by 103 (3 % ≈ 980 mM) — do not propagate. Recommended uric-acid BV set: ntotal=2, nα=1, α=0.46–0.5, k0 fitted to dEp/dlog v ≈ 60 mV/dec and Ep−Ep/2 ≈ 100 mV.

5 · Bench plan (test schematics)

Two benches, in order. Bench A characterizes the electrode model alone and is what the model exploration already runs; Bench B puts the rev-2 channel in the loop and is the source of the paper figure.

5.1 · Bench A — electrode model, ideal potentiostat

Ideal vsource (triangle, vpwl) applies E = V(re)−V(we) directly across the model; icell read by iprobe. Sweep ±0.6 V about the formal potential at 50/100/200 mV/s. Purpose: per-analyte i–E validation curves, no AFE artifacts. Already realized as the raw-Spectre netlists of §3 (electrode_models/net/, no OA needed) — an OA/Maestro version of Bench A is optional. Note the existing randles_electrode and tb_anatop_electrode_randles cells are abandoned stubs (2 res + 1 cap, no DUT, no maestro view) — ignore them; do not treat them as precedent. The real, in-use electrode is randles_cell (CE/RE/WE; params r_ct/r_u/r_ce/c_dl), instantiated as ECS in the pixel bench.

5.2 · Bench B — AFE in the loop (paper figure): copy of tb_anatop_pixel/PotFbTB

Recon verdict (2026-08-21): the simulate-ready DUT is the flat pixel bench tb_anatop_pixel, view PotFbTB, test ps_cv — class-AB anatop_tia_amp_ab as both A1 and A2, randles_cell as ECS, ideal R_F, real anatop_biasgen_local, tran stop=120m errpreset=conservative temp=37, already validated headless (1/1 completed., i_peak 2.05e-05). Recipe: copy view PotFbTB→CvBvTB, swap only the ECS master randles_cell→electrode_bc, keep the wiring. Optionally swap R_F→anatop_tia_rprog (A=V_OUT, B=FB; 10 control bits from an ncode variable exactly as tb_anatop_tia_rprog drives them) — never simulated in-loop, budget a debug pass.
Do NOT target the assembled blocks. anatop_pstat: its anatop_tia contains only the amp — no feedback element, tia_gain<15:0> floating (stored check marker) — and both amps are the superseded folded-cascode/bts_pmos pair. anatop_channel: never netlisted; stored check errors include an RE/WE label collision (would short the electrode) and vdd_25/vdd_10/vss_10 shorts. Fixing these is TIA integration work, tracked separately.

Sign convention (measured; the simplan TB-07 formula is inverted): vout = V_CM + iwe·rf, so imeas = (v("/V_OUT") − VAR("V_CM"))/VAR("rf"). Current ps_cv stimulus: triangle 0.75→1.75→0.75 V over 100 ms (i.e. 20 V/s), rf=35k, rct_we=1M cdl_we=1u. For the figure, re-center the sweep about vcm to exercise both polarities and slow it per §7.

Plotted set mirrors the old figure (Vpattern; icell/vout) but the money panel is the i–E voltammogram with per-analyte peaks of both signs. One curve per analyte from the same netlist with {E0′, n, k0, D, Cbulk} as design variables — but note the headless export flattens an enabled local sweep to its last value: run analytes as corners or separate invocations, not a local sweep.

5.3 · Maestro setup (house style)

  • Transient, tt_25 only for the figure; scan rate and vertex potentials as design variables (cv_v0, cv_v1, scan_rate), never literals in expressions.
  • save=selected with explicit save on every net an expression reads (vout, vfb, icell ammeter, vtri) — a multi-second transient at allpub repeats the 40 GB MC mistake.
  • Named intermediate outputs (i_cell, e_applied), scalars built on them (ip_anodic = ymax(i_cell), ip_cathodic = ymin(i_cell), ep_anodic = xval at ymax); avoid cross().
  • Timescale: 100 mV/s over ±0.6 V is a 24 s transient. Run the analog path unclocked (SAR out of the loop, as rev-1's figure did); if SAR sampling must be shown, decimate in post, do not simulate 24 s of 1 MHz clocking. Alternatively compress to 1–2 V/s and say so in the caption — open decision §7.
  • Headless: skill/eis_headless_run.sh -c <cell> -v maestro -t ps_cv -M 7200 — transient path validated (existing simruns/tb_anatop_pixel_cv_1M). Maestro setup by clone-and-retarget, never from scratch: skill/castalia_tia_tb_maestro.py (copy the maestro view, drop the other six tests) then rprog_maestro_setup.py-style textual splices for variables/models. New variables go in the setup-level <vars> (per-test vars never reach the netlister). Model libs are per-test with no API: patch active.state AND the highest-numbered test_states/Interactive.<N>.state identically. Close every Virtuoso before running; verify from the executed run's input.scs (ahdl_include path, ECS master + params, tran line, model section). Archive to ~/chips/castalia/simarchive/.
  • The pixel setup inherits save=allpub currents=all — switch to save=selected + explicit saves before any long transient.

6 · Library integration recipe (recon verified 2026-08-21)

6.1 · How Verilog-A cells work in this library

anatop_eis_zcpe is the precedent, and it was made by a plain cp -r of leon's cellview directory (byte-identical, no SKILL script; dbCopyCellView cannot open a veriloga view). Cell discovery is by directory scan — a filesystem clone is a valid add. The load-bearing pieces: the cell-level data.dm CDF (moduleName, namePrefix "ahdl", termOrder, parameterList) must agree with the module in veriloga/veriloga.va; veriloga/netlist.oa is generated by the importer and carries portOrder. Netlisting emits ahdl_include "<abs path>/veriloga.va" plus a subckt-style instance line keyed on moduleName, with parameters passed via the CDF parameterList (the busset6 instance in the executed CV netlist is the working example). The zcpe module/cell name mismatch is a legacy artefact — name the new module identically to its cell.

6.2 · Creating electrode_bc

Preferred, GUI/CIW (batch has never created a veriloga view here): launch with ANATOP_CELL/ANATOP_SYM unset; File→New→Cellview, lib castalia, view veriloga; module electrode_bc, terminals CE RE WE, every biomarker knob a parameter real; save — the importer writes netlist.oa/data.dm/master.tag and rewrites the cell CDF; accept auto symbol generation. Verify without trusting return values: ddGetObj("castalia" "electrode_bc" "symbol" "symbol.oa") non-nil; cdfGetBaseCellCDF(...)~>parameters lists the knobs; strings <cell>/data.dm | grep moduleName matches the .va.

Batch fallback: cp -r anatop_eis_zcpe electrode_bc, write the new .va, then ahdlUpdateCDBAView + ahdlSymbolGen in batch virtuoso (ahdlSck context exists in IC618 but is untested here; a stale portOrder/termOrder against a changed port list is a silent-wrong-netlist failure). If the clone's vp/vn ports are kept and only parameters added, the OA side needs no regeneration — cdfCreateParam + cdfSaveCDF suffices.

6.3 · Traps that apply

  • ic/castalia is unversioned — snapshot the cell dir (and tb_anatop_pixel/) before edits. Never make ingest castalia.
  • Fresh Virtuoso session per build; exit(0) + errset in batch; unbalanced paren = silent full-timeout hang; foreach(t …) throws (t protected); dbClose required, lib data.dm written only on clean exit; delete .cdslck/.oa- before diagnosing save failures (currently no .cdslck in the lib; ~20 stale .oa- under unrelated tb_anatop_eis_* cells).
  • Verilog-A modelling: use $limexp() for the BV exponentials (else conservative transients stall); give the diffusion branch a finite DC path (a pure Warburg/laplace_nd is singular at DC and a CV ramp is effectively DC); a Verilog-A element carries no mismatch statistics — it contributes nothing to MC and must never be the thing MC measures.
  • techGetTechLibName("castalia") = cdsDefTechLib: harmless for netlisting/sim, re-attach before layout.
  • Do not pkill virtuoso — the owner's interactive session may be live.

8 · References for the paper (survey complete 2026-08-21)

Novelty finding worth exploiting: no CMOS-potentiostat paper in the literature overlays a simulated CV from a physically modelled cell onto the circuit simulation — circuit papers simulate into equivalent circuits and separately measure real CVs. The co-simulated per-analyte CV is a contribution, not a reproduction. Closest precedent (cite it): Jog et al., IEEE SENSORS 2024 (Verilog-A 3-electrode model with kinetics + mass transport for co-simulation with interface circuits).
