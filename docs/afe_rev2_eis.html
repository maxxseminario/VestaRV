1 · What the engine is

One galvanostatic impedance-measurement chain, shared by all four AFE channels through a 4-terminal analog MUX, per the frozen CA0 decisions (time-multiplexed only, one engine, shared 4-rail guarded EIS bus). The chain forces a chopped bipolar current between CE and WE and synchronously demodulates the voltage sensed between RE and WE. A 3-electrode potentiostat cell is already Kelvin-shaped — RE senses without carrying current — so the 4-probe instrument maps onto 3 electrodes by tying I− and V− at WE (Config A of the impedance doc). The engine keeps four electrode terminals so Config B (a distinct RE2 sense electrode, already in the CQ1 pad budget) is a MUX-map change, not a schematic change.

ExcitationChopped current, 160 nA – 15 µA (5-bit amplitude B<4:0>, 3-bit range T<2:0>), differential Ioutp/Ioutn
SenseCurrent-balanced IA, high-Z inputs, chopper-stabilised, differential out
DetectionSquare-wave synchronous demodulation; Re/Im by 0°/90° demod-phase selection (two-pass)
BasebandPGA ×1–10 (diff → single-ended) then Sallen-Key LPF (2 kHz family, 60 dB stop-band)
DigitisationThe selected channel's SAR via the adcSourceSel mux pattern — no new ADC
Referencesvcm_eis and DC bias from the existing R-2R DACs (anatop_dac); leon's reference generators were deliberately not copied
Digital sideAlready built and verified: EIS registers at 0x7C00 (hart-0-only), IRQ source 56
Provenance caveat. Leon's assembled AFE cell is mode-700 and unreadable; the chain order used here is inferred from port signatures (§4) and has not been diffed against leon's actual top-level wiring. Everything below the engine top is a verbatim copy of leon leaf/mid cells. Ask Auerling for read access to leon/AFE; until then the order PGA-before-LPF is our design decision, argued in §4.

Global-net collision. Leon's bias doctrine mirrors rev-1's: the biasgen output pins drive global nets, and every block picks bias up globally, which is why anatop_eis_cbia has no bias terminals. Leon's globals are bnm! bnc! bpm! bpc!; our AFE island drives bn! bnc! bp! bpc!. bnc! and bpc! collide. Any cellview that contains both bias generators (the chip top, and every combined AFE-plus-EIS testbench) will short the two cascode-bias rails together. Resolution options, decide before the engine top is drawn: rename the EIS globals (eis_bnc!…) in the copied cells, or lift the four bias nets to explicit pins on the engine boundary. Renaming touches every copied cell once; pinning touches only the blocks that consume bias.

4 · Engine schematic — blocks, pins, nets

Chain order is fixed by the port signatures of the copied cells: the CBIA output is differential, the demod chopper is differential-in/differential-out, the PGA is the only differential-to-single-ended element, and the LPF is single-ended. The only type-consistent order is therefore CBIA → demod → PGA → LPF. This also puts the chopper ripple through two attenuators (PGA bandwidth, then the LPF) before the ADC.

6 · Clocking, demodulation, amplitude rule

  • Excitation is a square wave. The IDAC commutates a DC current at ck_ex; there is no sine source. Square-in/square-out synchronous detection responds to odd harmonics (1/n² weighted). Calibration against the on-chip R and R∥C removes the fixed harmonic-weighting error; residual error from the cell's dispersion across harmonics is small for Randles-type spectra and is accepted (leon shipped this way).
  • Re/Im by two passes. Demodulate once with ck_dem in phase with ck_ex (Re), once shifted 90° (Im). The phase select is a digital mux on ck_dem; no analog quadrature path. Whether leon does I/Q on chip is unknown (AFE unreadable) — two-pass is self-sufficient regardless.
  • Demod ripple vs f_ex. The demodulator output carries ripple at 2·f_ex. Above the LPF corner (f_ex ≳ 2 kHz) the SK filter removes it. Below, it passes — so for low-frequency points the firmware must average the SAR stream over an integer number of excitation periods. This is the standard cost of sub-corner EIS points and is why the sweep plan runs long records at 1–100 Hz.
  • Amplitude rule (small-signal validity). Keep the interface perturbation ≤ 10 mV: choose the IDAC code so |I|·|Z| ≤ 10 mV at the measurement frequency. Examples: |Z| = 100 kΩ → I ≤ 100 nA; |Z| = 1 kΩ → I ≤ 10 µA. Firmware picks the code from the previous (or a scout) magnitude estimate; the 160 nA–15 µA range covers |Z| ≈ 700 Ω – 60 kΩ at full 10 mV drive and larger |Z| at reduced SNR.
  • Chop vs excitation clocks are independent. ck_chop (CBIA offset/1-f suppression) can stay at a fixed frequency well above the signal band; only ck_dem must be phase-locked to ck_ex.

7 · Verification plan

Two layers. Layer 1 re-runs leon's own per-block benches on our copies — every one of them exists in leon with readable schematics and readable ADE XL state, so the setups are copied, repointed at the anatop_eis_* cells, and trusted as representative (they validated the silicon leon taped out). Layer 2 is system-level and is ours to build — leon has no readable assembled-chain bench.

Bench-view mechanics. Leon's TB cells have no view named "schematic" — each bench variant is its own named schematic view (e.g. CBIA_2p5V_TB carries TypOpenLoopWBiasTB, TypOpenLoopWBiasChopTB, CMGainWBiasTB, InputRangeWBiasTB), with one adexl view holding the tests. The copy script must name these views explicitly; castalia_eis_copy.il as written only handles schematic/symbol. See §8.

8 · Copying leon's benches and Maestro state

Feasibility verified on disk 2026-08-16: every Layer-1 bench schematic view and every adexl/maestro view listed above is world-readable. The copy is three steps:

  1. Named schematic views. Extend the castalia_eis_copy.il manifest with a per-cell view list (dbCopyCellView takes the view name — same call, more rows). Destination names: tb_anatop_eis_cbia etc., keeping leon's view names so the ADE state's view references survive.
  2. ADE XL / maestro views. These are dd data views, not OA cellviews — copy with ddCopyObj, not dbCopyCellView. Copy only data.sdb, test_states/…/*_active and states/; skip results/ (leon's numbers, gigabytes, not ours).
  3. Repoint the setups. The copied state still says leon:CBIA_2p5V_TB:1 inside data.sdb and the test states. Per the maestro-setup-file-layout finding these are hand-editable text: replace leon → castalia, source cell → tb_anatop_eis_* name, DUT cell → anatop_eis_* name. Model setup is untouched — leon uses the same crn65gplus_2d5_lk_v1d0.scs sections as the simplan.
Limits of "trust that these are representative". The per-block benches are representative — same PDK, same 2.5 V domain, and they qualified leon's silicon. Three things they cannot cover: (1) our chain order and the engine top (leon's AFE is unreadable — Layer 2 exists for this); (2) our DAC-driven references — leon's benches bias V_Ref/VCM from ideal sources, so E-09/E-10 must use anatop_dac outputs at least once; (3) our SAR interface and firmware averaging — leon digitised with AdcMim. Do not report a Layer-1 pass as engine verification.

10 · Option B — EIS through the existing chain: decision benches V-1…V-3

Step-by-step Maestro setup for these three benches (analyses, stimulus edits, variable tables, CSV entry of the outputs): afe_rev2_eis_benchsetup.html.

Added 2026-08-16 after the Layer-1 smoke run. Option B does EIS with no new analog: the AC pattern rides on v_pattern (A1 applies it to the cell), A2's TIA reads the AC current, the SAR samples the waveform, and I/Q demodulation is firmware. Accuracy then rests on three properties of the existing chain; V-1…V-3 measure them. If all three pass at the target frequency, Option B is frozen and the anatop_eis_* engine stays a library fallback.

Frequency target

Core spec0.1 Hz – 10 kHz, calibrated: |Z| ±1 %, phase ±1°
Extended (best effort)10 – 50 kHz, calibrated: ±5 %, ±5° — for the Rs plateau only
Single-frequency drift tracking1 kHz

Why 10 kHz: the fouling signature (Rct∥Cdl arc) sits at fc = 1/(2πRctCdl) ≈ 0.02–160 Hz for Rct 10 kΩ–1 MΩ, Cdl 0.1–10 µF — all diagnostic content is below ~1 kHz. The Rs plateau needs f ≳ 10/(2πRsCdl) ≈ 1.6–16 kHz for typical values; 10 kHz reaches it in most cases and fitting recovers Rs in the rest. Above ~50 kHz three limits stack for marginal information: the SAR (~1 MHz-class clock → ~80 kS/s, clean coherent sampling to a few tens of kHz), A2's RfCf corner (560 kΩ·150 pF → 1.9 kHz, 35 kΩ·150 pF → 30 kHz — high-frequency points must run at low Rf, which they naturally do since |Z|→Rs is small there), and the DAC update rate (a 10 kHz square is 20 k updates/s with a 5 µs settle budget; 50 kHz needs 1 µs).
