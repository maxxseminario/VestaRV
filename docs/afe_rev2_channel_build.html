1.2 What is superseded

anatop_controlamp (A1, folded cascode) anatop_tia_amp_ab The folded cascode sourced 14 µA and sank 3.5 µA; the class-AB cell delivers ±2.2 mA at 50 mV error and 53.27 dB of loop gain at 10 kHz into the cell.
anatop_tiaamp (A2, bts_pmos) anatop_tia_amp_ab 2/60 full-scale delivery under mismatch, and it is the source of the retired 15–38° phase-margin collapse.
tia_gain<15:0>, lb<5:0>, tb<5:0> ResEn<5:0>, ThEn<3:0> The 16-tap 35 kΩ string was never built. anatop_tia_rprog is built and measured: R = 18 kΩ + 6 kΩ·N, N = 0…319. lb/tb were the bts_pmos lead-compensation and tail trims and have no counterpart in the class-AB cell.

1.3 Session hygiene — do this before every editing session

  1. Confirm ANATOP_CELL and ANATOP_SYM are unset in the launching shell, or castalia_anatop_build.il will auto-build and exit.
  2. Launch Virtuoso from ~/chips/castalia/ic — cds.lib is resolved from the working directory, and launching from inside a view directory scaffolds a stray library tree there.
  3. Delete any sch.oa.cdslck* and sch.oa- files left by an aborted session before opening a cell; they silently block every later save.
  4. Use a fresh Virtuoso session per chapter. Re-running edits in a stale session drops individual terminals without an error.
  5. After any wire or label edit, run Check and Save (not Save). Connectivity is re-extracted by the checker; a bare save keeps the old instTerm-to-net binding.
  6. Never write into ~/chips/myshkin/ic or through a Castalia symlink into it.

3.2 Procedure

  1. Open castalia/anatop_pstat view schematic for edit.
  2. Select all and delete. In particular this removes I0 → anatop_controlamp and the incomplete bias stubs that left vbpc floating.
  3. Place castalia/anatop_tia_amp_ab; name it A1.
  4. Place two analogLib/cap, c = 500f; name them C_CMP1_CTRL and C_CMP2_CTRL.
  5. Place the rebuilt castalia/anatop_tia; name it I_TIA.
  6. Place castalia/anatop_tgate_p16 twice; name them SW_CE and SW_WE. These two carry the cell current; everything else here is minimum strength.
  7. Place castalia/anatop_tgate seven times; name them SW_RE, SW_LB, SW_CAL, SW_G1, SW_G2, SW_G3 and SW_GC (the mid-node clamp).
  8. Place castalia/anatop_inv; name it INV_G. It generates guard_enb locally, because anatop_tgate takes an active-high En and the clamp must close when the guard opens.
  9. Place castalia/anatop_tia_rprog; name it R_CAL.
  10. Place castalia/anatop_eis_ota_p; name it BUF_RE.
  11. Place two tsmcN65/rppolywo; name them R_TVO and R_TCE; set both to 1 kΩ (see §8, decision D-5, before committing the value).
  12. Draw the pins of §3.1.
  13. Wire per §3.3, then tie R_CAL's code bits per §3.4.
  14. Run Check and Save. Required: 0 errors, 0 warnings, and no floating-pin warning on any of the eleven control pins.

The RE buffer input is on the electrode side. BUF_RE.VInP connects to RE, not to re_amp. The A1 offset measurement (note_meas_ampab step 1) opens iso_re and closes lb_en; with the buffer on the amplifier side it would then report the loopback voltage instead of the electrode, and the measurement would be circular.
Switch sizing per path. SW_WE and SW_CE carry the cell current and use anatop_tgate_p16; their Ron drop is a potential error at the electrode, not a current error, because the current still passes through Rf by KCL. SW_RE carries no current and uses minimum-strength anatop_tgate, because off-state leakage on a reference electrode is the spec driver. See §8 decision D-2 for the Ron target.

3.4 The on-chip gain reference resistor (C4)

R_CAL is a second instance of anatop_tia_rprog with its code bus tied to a constant. This costs no new cell, guarantees the same unit device (rppolywo, w = 400 n, l = 2.71 µ) as the feedback ladder so sheet resistance cancels exactly, and leaves only matching — 0.04–0.36 %.

  1. Tie R_CAL.ThEn<3> and ThEn<2> to vss!.
  2. Tie R_CAL.ThEn<1> and ThEn<0> to vdd!.
  3. Tie R_CAL.ResEn<5> to vdd!.
  4. Tie R_CAL.ResEn<4:0> to vss!.

That is N = 128 + 32 = 160, Rcal = 978 kΩ nominal. Calibration takes two vref codes with A1 in loopback: Ical = Δvref / Rcal, Δvout = Ical·Rf, so the measured quantity is the ratio Rf/Rcal and the ±35 % sheet tolerance cancels. Δvref is scaled per gain code so the output never rails: 2.4 V at N = 0, 0.4 V at N = 319.

Slots 10 and 11 are a layout distinction, not a schematic one. The AFE bias rails are globals: in the schematic vbn_ch and chip-level slot 2 are the same net, and a netlist comparison will show them shorted. What separates them is where the wire is tapped. The layout must take slots 10/11 at the channel end of the bias route and the chip-level slots at the generator; otherwise the pair carries no information and the IR-drop measurement of note_meas_biasgen step 1 cannot be made. Annotate both taps in the schematic so the apparent redundancy is not optimised away. The same applies to vssa/vdda against the chip-level sense slots.

5 · Rebuild anatop_channel

5.1 The four stored errors, and what each one is

Stored message (verbatim)MeaningCorrect construction
Ignored label 'WE' because multiple labels exist on wire 'RE'. One wire carries both the RE and the WE label. Netlisted, this shorts the reference and working electrodes together — the loop would servo RE to vref while A2 holds the same node at vcm. One wire per electrode, one label per wire. CE, RE and WE must remain three distinct nets from the anatop_pstat instance to the three pins.
Short between 'vdd_25' and 'vdd_10'. The 2.5 V analog rail and the 1.0 V core rail are the same net. Two separate nets. vdd_25 reaches anatop_sar.VDDA25 and VDDD25; vdd_10 reaches anatop_sar.VDDD10 and anatop_testmux.vdd_10 only.
Short between 'vss_10' and 'vdd_10'. Supply shorted to ground. Nothing on the 1.0 V rail may share a wire with the ground net.
Ignored label 'vss_25' because multiple labels exist on wire 'vss_10'. Two ground labels on one wire. There is only one ground in this design — anatop_sar.VSSD and VSSA25 both return to it. Keep the single name vss_25, delete the vss_10 label and the vss_10 pin, and drive anatop_testmux.vss_10 from vss_25. Aliasing two names is what produced this error; removing one name removes the class of error.

6.2 New cell anatop_atbpad — the bus exit

  1. Create castalia/anatop_atbpad, view schematic.
  2. Place tsmcN65/rppolywo twice, 200 Ω each; name them R_ESD_D and R_ESD_O.
  3. Place castalia/anatop_tgate_p16; name it SW_DIR. It must carry force current, so it is the ×16 cell.
  4. Place the test buffer (see §8, decision D-1); name it BUF_ATB.
  5. Draw pins atb (inputOutput), pad_atb_d (inputOutput), pad_atb_o (output), DirEn, BufEn (input, 2.5 V logic).
  6. Wire pad_atb_d → R_ESD_D → atbd_x, then SW_DIR: A→atbd_x, B→atb, En→DirEn.

  8. Wire atbo_x → R_ESD_O → pad_atb_o.
  9. Check and Save (0/0); generate the symbol.
Both series resistors sit between the pad and everything else, so the buffer is inside the pad's resistor rather than outside it, and no 2.5 V thin-oxide switch is ever the CDM conduction path.

6.3 Rebuild anatop

  1. Open castalia/anatop view schematic; select all; delete.
  2. Place castalia/anatop_channel four times; name them CH0…CH3.
  3. Place castalia/anatop_biasgen; name it BIASGEN.
  4. Place castalia/anatop_dac once for vcm; name it DAC_VCM.
  5. Place castalia/anatop_dac four times for vref; name them DAC_VREF0…3. The existing cell has one shared vref DAC; the 2026-08-16 decision is one per site (§9 item 8).
  6. Place castalia/anatop_testmux_g; name it GMUX.
  7. Place castalia/anatop_atbpad; name it ATBPAD.
  8. Place castalia/anatop_tgate; name it SW_VCMISO. Place castalia/anatop_inv; name it INV_VCMISO.

  10. Wire DAC_VREF<n>.Out→vref<n>→CH<n>.vref.
  11. Wire the ATB bus: CH0…CH3.atb, GMUX.atb and ATBPAD.atb all to the single net atb.
  12. Wire ATBPAD.pad_atb_d and pad_atb_o to the two new top-level pins of the same names.
  13. Wire GMUX's slot inputs per §6.1.
  14. Check and Save (0/0); generate the symbol.
Arbitration is one net with five drivers, and only RTL keeps it safe. atb is a single bus reached by five grant T-gates. Under the orchestrator each AFE<h> block is owned by a different hart, so the AND of every per-site AFE_DBGEN with the hart-0 AFE_DBGOWNSEL decode must be hardware in the 0x7C00 block, not a software convention. The analog cells take one already-granted enable each; nothing in the schematic can prevent contention if that AND is omitted. Add the AFE_DBGKEY write key to the same block: a debug switch closing mid-measurement corrupts a clinical result silently.

8 · Open decisions

Each of these changes drawn geometry. None is chosen here.

#DecisionOptionsConsequence
D-1Test-buffer cell for atb_o (a) copy pingora2 AdcBufferOpamp — not in the castalia library today, needs its own copy script. (b) reuse anatop_tia_amp_ab, which is that cell's descendant (AdcBufferOpamp → SarAdcBufferOpamp → OpampRRIO → OpampAdcDriver) and is already characterised in this library. (c) a third instance of anatop_eis_ota_p. The buffer must cover bn! at 0.61 V and bp! at 1.82 V with ≤ 20 pA of input current and 50 pF of drive. anatop_tia_amp_ab is PMOS-input; confirm its input common-mode range actually reaches 1.82 V before committing (b), which is otherwise the cheapest by far.
D-2Ron of SW_WE (and SW_CE) (a) anatop_tgate, minimum, ≈ 1.5 kΩ → 61 mV at 40.7 µA. (b) anatop_tgate_p16, ≈ 190 Ω → 7.7 mV, ≈ 5 ADC LSB. (c) a parallel bank of anatop_tgate_p16 for ≈ 50 Ω. The drop is a shift in the applied cell potential, not a current error — the current still passes through Rf by KCL. Calibration stores it (slots 7 minus 8), so the driver is compliance and potential accuracy, not measurement accuracy. The 12 Ω that an uncorrected ¼-LSB would need is not achievable. This guide assumes (b).
D-3Bias domain for the RE buffer (a) AFE domain. Leave BUF_RE's inherited connections at their defaults; they already resolve to bn! bnc! bp! bpc!, which is what the AFE anatop_biasgen drives. Zero extra cells, zero extra globals, zero wiring. (b) EIS domain. Rename the island's globals to eis_bnm! eis_bnc! eis_bpm! eis_bpc!, instantiate one anatop_eis_biasgen, and override BUF_RE's inherited connections onto them. Naming is free either way, because the bias pins are inherited connections and can be pointed at any net at instantiation. What differs is the levels: the published E-02 / V-3x numbers (AOL 76.6 dB, UGB 3.51 MHz, offset −87.6 µV, 28.3 µA) were measured with anatop_eis_biasgen driving those nets. (a) changes the operating point and invalidates them until tb_anatop_rebuf is re-run with anatop_biasgen_local substituted; (b) preserves them and permanently resolves the bnc!/bpc! collision, at the cost of one more bias generator and four more globals inside the analog island.
D-4R_CAL code: tied or programmable (a) tie to N = 160 as in §3.4 — 0 register bits. (b) route the 10 code bits to registers — a per-code calibration current, at 10 more SWM bits per site. (a) is sufficient because Δvref is already programmable to 14 bits, which covers the whole gain range by scaling the calibration current instead of the resistor. (b) only buys accuracy at the bottom gain codes, which note_meas_tiarprog step 1 explicitly skips.
D-5Series isolation R on the vout and ce_amp taps (a) 1 kΩ. (b) larger, if ATB settling permits. 1 kΩ is the starting value from the debug plan. It must decouple ATB's ≈ 200 fF from A2's output without adding error, and it stacks with the mux tree's ≈ 5 kΩ and the pad's 200 Ω when sizing settling. Fix it from an ATB settling simulation, not by assumption.
D-6Compensation capacitor device (a) analogLib/cap, exactly as the reference bench. (b) tsmcN65/mimcap_sin sized to 500 fF. (a) reproduces the reference numbers bit-for-bit and is the right choice for the §7.2 equivalence run. (b) is mandatory before layout. Run the equivalence with (a), then swap and re-run, recording the delta.
D-7Pad cells for atb_d / atb_o (a) one PDB3A_G per signal, 25 µm of island each. (b) Myshkin's bond-pad-plus-companion-ESD-cell pattern, 50 µm each. The north analog island is 250 µm against 18 slots already claimed. If the companion pattern returns, the two ATB pads cost 100 µm rather than 50 µm and the ring cuts move further. Owned by the pad-ring owner, but it gates the floorplan estimate.
D-8The 200 kΩ vref source impedance against the SAR (a) extend the SAR's acquisition window for AFE_ADCSRCSEL = 2. (b) add a hold capacitor on vref_loc. (c) buffer the vref tap. (d) accept a code-independent gain error and calibrate it out. The 14-bit DAC presents ≈ 200 kΩ at every code. Into the SAR's sampling capacitor that is ≈ 200 ns per time constant, and ten-bit settling needs about seven of them — longer than one conversion period at 869.6 kS/s. The 4096-code sweep of note_meas_dacr2r12 step 3 depends on this being resolved.

9 · Conflicts found

Every item below is a disagreement between the approved slot map, a TRM measurement step, and what the cells in castalia actually contain. Resolve each before the schematic is frozen.

  1. AFE_ADCSRCSEL = 3 is labelled "RE" but must be re_buf. The debug plan §6.3 names code 3 "RE (the RE-tap fallback)", while its own C2 note rejects buffering RE into the converter because the sampling capacitor draws charge from the reference electrode, and §11.4 retires the RE tap in favour of a buffer at source. Routing the raw electrode to ADCMUX.A<3> would reinstate exactly the rejected structure. This guide wires A<3> to t_re_buf. The register-map prose should be corrected to say so.
  2. Chip-level slot 8, "iref_ovr", does not exist on anatop_biasgen. The cell's terminals are BiasAdj<5:0>, DacBN/DacBNC/DacBP/DacBPC<13:0>, EnBuf, EnGen, UseDac, Vdd10, Gnd10, Vdd25, Gnd25, ExtBP, ExtBPC, ExtBNC, ExtBN, UseExt — four separate external override inputs and a 14-bit DAC override per rail, not one reference-current node. note_meas_biasgen step 5 ("chip-level slot 8 drives the reference-current node") cannot be executed as written. §6.1 above remaps slots 8–11 onto ExtBN/ExtBNC/ExtBP/ExtBPC, forced one rail at a time with UseExt = 1; the TRM step needs the same rewording.
  3. Per-channel slots 10/11 are electrically identical to chip-level slots 2/4 in the schematic. The AFE bias rails are globals reached by inherited connections, so vbn_ch and bn! are one net. The distinction the slot map intends — generation versus distribution — exists only in layout, and only if the taps are physically placed at opposite ends of the bias route. A netlist-level equivalence check will report them shorted, correctly. Annotate the intent on both taps.
  4. The amux family still references teewinot. String inspection of anatop_amux_16 and anatop_amux_4 reports teewinot.MUX2A as an instance master, and anatop_amux_2 reports teewinot.TGP8A/TGN8A/TGP1A/TGN1A; anatop_mux2 reports the same set plus stored Unsync warnings. The debug plan §11.1 states the copy repointed all headers and verified "0 foreign-library references". OA string tables are append-only and retain stale masters, so this is not proof — but it must be settled before the muxes are used, because teewinot is another user's live home directory. Check: netlist anatop_amux_16 and grep the result for teewinot; if it appears, run the repoint pass again over the schematic and layout hierarchies.
  5. anatop_biasgen's children were never repointed. Its sub-masters still resolve through AFE_ECS (BiasGenCascWideSwing, Dac2V5R2R14, DacPowerSupply, MUX2A, OpAmpCurrentMirrorP, TGN16A, TGP16A) rather than onto the anatop_* copies, unlike every other repaired cell. That is legal — the library is single-homed by design — but it means editing the chip bias generator means editing frozen Myshkin collateral. Copy the sub-cells explicitly before touching them.
  6. VddDac has no cell to tap. Chip-level slot 7 requires the DAC supply node, but anatop_dacsupply does not exist in the library; the supply appears only as AFE_ECS/DacPowerSupply inside anatop_biasgen and as the separate bench cell anatop_pixel_dac_psu. Either expose the node as a terminal on anatop_biasgen, or promote anatop_pixel_dac_psu to a real block. Until then note_meas_dacr2r12 step 4 — the only on-silicon confirmation of the 25.8 mV excursion — has no path.
  7. The C1/C2/C4 control bits are not in the approved register map. §6.1 of the debug plan allocates nine bits (AFE_DBGSEL[3:0], AFE_DBGEN, AFE_FRCSEL[6:5], AFE_FRCEN, AFE_DBGISOEN) and reserves 31:9. The rebuilt anatop_pstat needs six more per site — IsoCe, IsoRe, IsoWe, LbEn, RcalEn, BufEn. Proposed: AFE_ISOCE bit 9, AFE_ISORE 10, AFE_ISOWE 11, AFE_LBEN 12, AFE_RCALEN 13, AFE_BUFEN 14, keeping 31:15 reserved. This is within C5's "8–10 control bits" only if C5 is read as the observe-path bits alone; it is 15 in total.
  8. anatop has one shared vref DAC. The built top level instantiates anatop_dac twice — once for vcm, once for vref — while CLAUDE.md records the 2026-08-16 decision as per-channel vref so that each site runs an independent potential program. Chapter 6 assumes per-channel. If the shared DAC is kept, four sites cannot hold four different formal potentials, and the four different Rf taps of the marker panel lose their purpose.
  9. The force path is the observation mux itself. The register map defines AFE_FRCSEL and AFE_FRCEN as if dedicated force switches exist. They are not needed: the mux tree is transmission gates and passes current in both directions, so any slot can be driven from atb_d. AFE_DBGISOEN and AFE_VCMISOEN already release the DAC. Either delete AFE_FRCSEL/AFE_FRCEN, or define them as a safety interlock that merely qualifies AFE_DBGDIREN. Note that forcing through the mux puts ≈ 5 kΩ of tree plus 200 Ω of pad resistance in series, which is harmless for vref/vcm — they carry no DC current — but is an error term for the bias-override slots 8–11.
  10. Triage step 6 reads slot 6 on the buffered pad. §9 of the debug plan predates §11: it says "DBGSEL = RE, buffered", but slot 6 is already the RE buffer output, so atb_o stacks two amplifier offsets of the same class on a measurement whose tolerance is ±16 mV. Read slot 6 on atb_d.
  11. Sign convention. The strategy document gives vout = vcm − Iwe·Rf; the pixel bench settled empirically on vout = V_CM + iwe·rf for its source convention, and the CV figure script uses anodic-positive = (V_CM − V_OUT)/rf. All three are the same circuit with different current-direction conventions. State one convention in the TRM and in firmware; the bench expression cv_i = (VT("/V_OUT") - VAR("V_CM")) / VAR("rf") is the one the archived numbers were produced with.
  12. Every TRM measurement step is served by this design except two. Items 2 and 6 above are the only steps that cannot be executed as written — note_meas_biasgen step 5 and note_meas_dacr2r12 step 4. Everything else maps: note_meas_adc 1–4 to ADCMUX.A<4>, A<1>, the vcm DAC code and slot 9; note_meas_ampab 1–4 to lb_en/iso_re, slots 5 and 4, and the per-site vref DAC; note_meas_dacr2r12 1–3 to slot 2, SW_VREFISO and ADCMUX.A<2> (subject to D-8); note_meas_pixel 1–5 to slots 5, 6, 7 and iso_we; note_meas_tiarprog 1–4 to slot 4, slots 7/8 and R_CAL/rcal_en.
